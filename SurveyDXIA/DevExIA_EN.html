Understanding the Impact of AI on the Developer Experience

This survey is conducted by Karina Kohl Silveira at the Institute of Informatics at UFRGS. Its aim is to understand how the use of AI tools affects feedback cycles, cognitive load, and flow state in software developers’ everyday work, as well as their perceived impact on creativity, stress, and attention.

The questionnaire is anonymous, can be stopped at any time, and responses are used exclusively for academic research purposes.

I, Karina Kohl Silveira, researcher at the Institute of Informatics at UFRGS and responsible for the study “Understanding the Impact of AI on the Developer Experience (DevEx): Feedback Loops, Cognitive Load, Flow State, and Their Interactions with Creativity, Stress, and Attention,” invite you to participate as a volunteer in this study.

This research aims to examine how the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools affects the three core dimensions of developer experience (DevEx): feedback cycles, cognitive load, and flow state. It will also explore the perceived effects of AI on subjective aspects such as creativity, stress, and attention, offering a broader view of developers’ experience in an increasingly AI-mediated environment. We believe this study is important because it seeks to provide both quantitative and qualitative evidence from real-world data, analyzed through methodologically rigorous approaches.

Your participation will involve completing this questionnaire. Some discomfort may arise while answering it, although the study presents minimal risk to participants. As defined by CNS Resolution No. 510/2016, Article 2, Item XXV, risk refers to the possibility of harm to a person’s physical, psychological, moral, intellectual, social, or cultural well-being at any stage of the research and as a consequence of it.

In the context of this study, the main identified risks are psychological and emotional, and there may be embarrassment, stress, fatigue, or discomfort while completing the questionnaire due to the nature of some questions involving subjective aspects of professional experience with the use of AI tools.

To minimize these risks, the following precautionary measures are adopted:

  • The questionnaire is anonymous and can be interrupted at any time. You are guaranteed the right not to accept participation or to withdraw your permission at any time, without any penalty or retaliation, by your decision, as provided in CNS Resolution No. 510/2016, Art. 9º, II;
  • The collected data will be treated confidentially and used exclusively for scientific research purposes;
  • The study does not involve collection of sensitive data such as medical information, sexual orientation, or banking data;
  • In case of any harm resulting from participation in the research, you will be entitled to appropriate assistance and compensation, as determined by Brazilian law (CNS Resolution No. 510/2016, Art. 19, §2º).

It is expected that the results of this research will contribute to a deeper and evidence-based understanding of the effects of using Artificial Intelligence tools on the experience of developers, especially considering subjective and psychosocial aspects often neglected in the literature.

Throughout the research period you have the right to clarify any questions or request any further clarification, simply by contacting the research lead, Karina Kohl Silveira, by phone/WhatsApp +55 51 98422-0770, or email: karina.kohl@inf.ufrgs.br. If you have any questions about your rights as a research participant, contact the Research Ethics Committee of the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (CEP/UFRGS) by phone/WhatsApp +55 51 3308-3787, email: cep@pucrs.br, Monday to Friday from 8am to 12pm and 1:30pm to 5pm. The Ethics Committee is an independent body composed of professionals from different areas of knowledge and community members. Its responsibility is to guarantee the protection of participants’ rights, safety, and well-being through review and approval of the study, among other actions.

If you incur expenses due to your participation in this study related to transportation and/or food, you will be appropriately reimbursed by the researchers.

The information in this research is confidential and will be disclosed only in scientific events or publications, with no identification of participants, except among those responsible for the study, and confidentiality regarding your participation is assured.
